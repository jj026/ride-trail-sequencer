## How to read this

- **mph** is moving speed; time spent stopped doesn't count.
- **Climbing** and **±ft** only count while moving, so altimeter drift during stops doesn't show up as climbing. They come from the device and are roughly ±5%.
- **Heart rate** is what the device recorded, averaged over each trail.
- **Rating** is OpenStreetMap's `mtb:scale:imba` tag; *unrated* means nobody has tagged the trail yet.
